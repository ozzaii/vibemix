<objective>
Harden the controller-state primitives and PROVE FLX4 decode deterministically — building on the existing, already-tested MIDI subsystem (do NOT rebuild it). Three deliverables: (1) close the stale-moves hardening gap so an unplug leaves no recent-move readable by reactions (`mark_disconnected` today clears only the connected flag, not the rings); (2) pin the live binding/decode path as canonical on `midi/profiles/` and document that `midi/controllers/`+`MidiMapLoader` is a separate, currently-unwired registry; (3) prove the FLX4 decode end-to-end with a live-shaped synthetic MIDI stream, and pin the unknown-controller graceful path bind→decode→no-crash.

Purpose: this is the trustworthy controller-input seam Phases 54/55 (hype/coach) consume. If stale moves leak after an unplug, or the decode silently binds the wrong map, every controller-grounded reaction is hallucinated. The bar is "the AI only ever reads controller moves that physically happened, from the right controller." This plan is Wave 1 — it makes the primitives correct + proven; Plan 02 (Wave 2) wires hot-plug detection into the live session and adds the disconnect→reconnect integration proof.

Output: a 2-line additive source change to `mark_disconnected` + three/four test files (two new, two extended). No decode-path source is modified — the golden FLX4 decode stays byte-equivalent.
</objective>

<grounding_facts>
- `mark_disconnected` is NOT called anywhere on the `handle_msg` decode path — it is only called by the disconnect callback (`_midi_common.handle_port_change` :251). So clearing the rings inside it cannot affect golden decode.
- `midi/controllers/` + `MidiMapLoader` (map_loader.py) have ZERO importers in `src/vibemix/` outside their own module — verified by grep this session. They are a parallel, unwired registry. Do NOT switch the live path to them; do NOT delete them.
- `_moves` dedups same-label-within-400ms and trims >12s; `_events` trims >12s. Both are time-based, so after an unplug (no new events) they sit frozen with up-to-12s of stale moves until cleared — which is exactly why mark_disconnected must clear them.
- The bipolar default for xfader/tempo/filter is 64; `deck_snapshot()` starts vol=0, eq/filter/tempo=64.
</grounding_facts>
