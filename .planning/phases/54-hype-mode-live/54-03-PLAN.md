<objective>
Pin the anti-slop spine for hype mode: every hype reaction must trace to a real Event + real evidence, and when the evidence is empty/weak the system stays silent rather than hallucinating a hype line. This is vibemix's central product principle ([[project_anti_slop_grounded_gemini_thesis]]) and the hallucination-grounding hard gate (CLAUDE.md release blocker) — this plan makes it a regression, not a hope.

Critical grounding (54-RESEARCH Q2): grounding lives in AICoach.build_prompt (evidence_line from REAL MusicState + per-event task) and is ENFORCED at emit by the CitationLinter chokepoint — a reaction citing an event not in the EvidenceRegistry is STRIPPED (no voice), reason 'invalid_atoms'. The detector itself refuses to fire on silence/weak audio. This plan pins BOTH gates with REAL primitives (EvidenceRegistry + CitationLinter, no network, no live LLM) plus the HYPE-persona-built-from-real-state contract.

Output: an anti-slop test (detector floor + linter spine) + a HYPE-persona-grounding test. No source change — the strip-on-unbacked-citation behavior is PINNED, not altered.
</objective>
