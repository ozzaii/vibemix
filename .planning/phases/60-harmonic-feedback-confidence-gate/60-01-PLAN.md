Verified circle-of-fifths derivation (60-RESEARCH §Pattern 1, lines 184-263).
Same-letter hour-distance → pitch-class semitone distance → verdict:
  - hour 0 → 0 semitones (same key)        → SAFE   (_SAFE_HOURS)
  - hour 1 → perfect fifth (5/7 st)         → SAFE   (adjacent ±1 bread-and-butter)
  - hour 2 → 2 semitones (+2 energy boost)  → SAFE
  - hour 3 → 3 semitones                    → NEITHER (drift; is_clash False, compatible False, STAY SILENT)
  - hour 4 → 4 semitones                    → NEITHER (drift; STAY SILENT)
  - hour 5 → 1 SEMITONE                      → CLASH  (_CLASH_HOURS)
  - hour 6 → 6 semitones (tritone)           → CLASH
  - hour 7 → 1 SEMITONE                      → CLASH
Tables: `_CLASH_HOURS = frozenset({5, 6, 7})` · `_SAFE_HOURS = frozenset({0, 1, 2})`.
Cross-letter same-number (`na==nb`, different letter) = relative major/minor = SAFE.
Hour-distance is circular: `min(|n1-n2| % 12, 12 - |n1-n2| % 12)`, range 0..6.
