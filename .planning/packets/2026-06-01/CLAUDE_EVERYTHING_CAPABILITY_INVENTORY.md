## 1. State of the product

vibemix is a real, deeply-built AI DJ co-host whose **perception → event → grounded-prompt → reaction spine is the strongest part of the system** — single-writer `MusicState`, a typed event detector with per-type cooldowns, an append-only `EvidenceRegistry`, a response-level `CitationLinter`, the slop filter, and the live Vibe Judge are all source-correct and broadly unit-proven, with a handful of genuinely end-to-end proofs (real-CLAP centered-cosine retrieval, heuristic cue detection on real audio, the MOSS ONNX engine against cached weights, and a live-Gatekeeper-accepted signed DMG). The Tauri shell folds five surfaces into one keep-alive window with no dead buttons. **But three systemic gaps gate any "a stranger hears the co-host" claim:** (1) the single most consequential honest finding across all silos is that **the shipped product cannot speak on any machine but Kaan's** — MOSS is neither bundled in the DMG nor auto-downloadable (no hosted URL), so a fresh machine boots voice-muted, even though multiple silos correctly label the MOSS *code* as proven; (2) the shipped DMG is **18 src-touching commits stale** and **arm64-only** (no Intel/Universal2 artifact despite the CI matrix claim); and (3) almost every "live" capability — the Judge firing on real deck audio, every "live demo credits a skill" path, the recall path, the Viber Codex-exec runs, deck-pair grounding — is **unit/synthetic-proven only, never captured on a real rig**. Two whole feature families are honestly absent behind real-looking consumers: the **v11 beatmatching producer** (`BEATMATCH_GRADED` has a wired consumer but no emitter) and the **Mixxx H1-H8 / DB / OSC integration lanes** (the math ports are real and tested but every live producer is unbuilt). MOSS-only TTS enforcement holds with zero active cloud/fake spoken fallback; the residual cloud-TTS seams (Gemini-native TTS class, OpenRouter monkey-patch, Cartesia config) are dead import-only residue flagged for deletion.
