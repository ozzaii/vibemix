The maps that say these are NOT-FOUND are stale by hours. **BUT** — and this is the synthesizer's key correction — lens 1 overstated the recovery: the **10-dimension `transition_scorer`** (the *good* transition engine) is **still orphaned** to the pill + Viber, NOT in either coach loop (verified: zero hits for `transition_scorer`/`score_transition_slate` in `runtime/coach.py` + `state/coach.py`). Only the *shallow* 2-signal judge is live. The genuine still-lost residue: the **practice-deck loop** (unlocks Mastered beatmatching), the **R-SLOP grounding siblings** (`lufs.py`/`loop_geometry.py` absent, `xfade.py` unwired), the **universal library parsers** (only `rekordbox.py` exists), and the **debrief recap PNG** (no producer).

2. **The biggest product gap is on the REACTION side, and no map names it:** the live coach **never speaks a track recommendation** (`SuggestionService`/`next_suggestion` absent from `coach.py`) and is **blind to the set you prepped in Viber** (`built_set`/`set_plan` absent). Both engines already run silently — the *voice* just never reads them. Same orphan-disease as the EQ keystone, but on the output side.

3. **The marketing claims more than the code delivers, and one of those is a live code BUG:** the voice picker ships 8 retired-Gemini voice names that all fall through to ONE wrong MOSS voice (`local_tts.py:276` `voices[0]` fallback; default `"kore"` at `config_store.py:169` does not exist in MOSS). Screen-grounding ("reads track/BPM/key/cue points on screen") is in the partner brief but **disabled by default** (`dj_cohost.py:2199`).

**The five highest-leverage moves (§6) split cleanly: 2 are pure wiring of already-built engines, 1 is the practice-deck build, 1 is a copy/voice-picker honesty fix, 1 is the universal-parser island. None requires inventing new intelligence.**
