<objective>
The cue-anchored excerpt refinement slice. Plan 01 embeds whole tracks; this
slice replaces that with cue-anchored ≤80s windows so the embedding represents
the parts the DJ actually mixes on (intro mix-in, drop, breakdown) — exactly the
RESEARCH-confirmed best anchors. DJ-library cues become `source="dj"`
`CueAnchor`s; un-cued tracks fall back to the offline `detect_cues()` engine
(`source="auto"`) so no track is ever left anchor-less (the coverage risk the
research flagged). `excerpt.py` is the single consumer that turns anchors into
audio windows; `ingest.py` is rewired to mean-pool the per-window CLAP vectors.

Purpose: close the "embed the mixable moments, not arbitrary slices" gap — the
direct enabler of "enter on hot cue 2" retrieval downstream — while honoring
the XML-first / DSP-fallback / trust-the-audio contract.

Output: `library/excerpt.py` (anchor mapping + window cutting), the ingest
rewiring, and offline tests. Depends on Plan 01 (ingest.py exists) and Plan 02
(cue Type fidelity — the dj-anchor mapping needs the real Type label).
</objective>
