# v2.1 Phase Detail Archive — 2026-05-27

This archive collapses the expanded v2.1 phase-detail tree out of active
planning search surfaces.

## Why Archived

The files under `v2.1-phases/` are historical implementation plans and
summaries from phases 27-39. Several plans contain retired product guidance,
including Gemini Embedding 2 library search, Gemini-only/no-CLAP constraints,
old budget assumptions, and pre-current installer/updater wording. Keeping the
expanded tree under `.planning/milestones/` made active sweeps noisy and risked
future agents following obsolete decisions.
