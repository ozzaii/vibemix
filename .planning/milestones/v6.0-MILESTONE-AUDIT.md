## Milestone Goal Achievement

> **Goal:** Shift vibemix from a *reactive* co-host to a *forward-leaning* **copilot**. Mechanism = memory: every session feeds an embedding store; coach prompts ground in past sessions. Personalization is emergent from the retrieval seam — not configured, not LLM-extracted. New headline hallucination class — **retrieval poisoning** — is the anti-slop release gate (P65), and retrieved moments are citable-by-construction.

**Goal achieved at engineering level.** The retrieval seam is structurally anti-poisoning by construction (existence-only `recall` source flows through existing `CitationLinter` with zero new linter code; fabricated `[recall:<id>]` strips the whole turn; PAST-tense fence; current-session excluded; ~0.7 similarity floor; top-k 2-3 cap; event-gated). The copilot voice carries no anti-features (no next-track rec, no extracted tendencies, no settings personalization, no continuous audio embed). Both visible copilot moves (transition-shape callback + vocabulary callback) bottom out in real registered past moments via the linter.

**Felt-quality discharge** (whether the callbacks FEEL real on Kaan's real DJ corpus) is the §RECALL-EAR KAAN-ACTION carry-forward, with default `VIBEMIX_RECALL_ENABLED=0` until passed — mirroring the Phase 60 harmonic veto pattern.
