**Step 7 — Cmd+Tab** between Deck window and Learn window — both should remain responsive; neither should drop its ws connection (verifiable in ui.log: no `[vmx:error] ws closed` between the two switches).

**Step 8 — Disconnect** the FLX4 (yank the USB). The unplugged-toast should appear at the top of the Learn window for 4 s; the SVG should return to neutral inactive strokes; reconnecting should re-mount the FLX4 SVG within 2 s.

**Step 9 — Read ui.log:** Per `feedback_debug_live_app_via_ui_log`:
```bash
tail -100 "/Users/ozai/Library/Application Support/world.bravoh.vibemix/vibemix/logs/ui.log" | grep "vmx:ipc"
```
Expected: a stream of `[vmx:ipc<] ipc.learn.midi_position` lines during knob twisting (delta-suppressed), exactly 1 `[vmx:ipc<] ipc.learn.controller_detected` line on plug-in, exactly 1 on disconnect, and ZERO `[vmx:error] ws closed` lines during the session.
