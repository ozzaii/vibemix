## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship relating to **vibemix**, an open-source AI co-host for DJs built by Bravoh, and adjacent commercial collaborations (the "**Purpose**"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other certain non-public, proprietary, or confidential information.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient, in any form (oral, written, visual, electronic, or otherwise), whether or not marked as confidential, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

- product roadmaps, unreleased features, technical architecture, source code, and model behavior;
- commercial terms, pricing, partner lists, customer information, and go-to-market plans;
- financial information, fundraising plans, and investor materials;
- user data, telemetry, and any data derived from the operation of vibemix;
- the existence, contents, and status of the discussions between the Parties.

Confidential Information does **not** include information that the Recipient can demonstrate by written record:

1. was rightfully in the Recipient's possession without obligation of confidentiality prior to disclosure by the Discloser;
2. is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the Recipient;
3. was rightfully received by the Recipient from a third party not under a duty of confidentiality; or
4. was independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations of the Recipient

The Recipient agrees to:

1. use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
2. protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care;
3. limit access to the Confidential Information to those of its employees, officers, directors, advisors, and contractors who (a) have a legitimate need to know for the Purpose and (b) are bound by written obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those of this Agreement; and
4. not disclose, publish, copy, reverse-engineer, or otherwise exploit the Confidential Information except as expressly permitted by this Agreement.

The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its personnel or representatives.

## 4. Compelled Disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, regulation, court order, or governmental authority to disclose Confidential Information, the Recipient shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Discloser prompt prior written notice so that the Discloser may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, and shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information legally required.

## 5. No License; Open-Source Carve-Out

Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any right, license, or interest in or to the Confidential Information, except the limited right to use it for the Purpose. All Confidential Information remains the sole property of the Discloser.

For the avoidance of doubt, the vibemix source code published under the Apache License 2.0 in the public repository at `github.com/bravoh-ai/vibemix` is **not** Confidential Information of Bravoh. This Agreement does not restrict either Party's rights under the Apache 2.0 license. Bravoh's unreleased roadmap, internal models, commercial plans, and unpublished branches remain Confidential Information.

## 6. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for a period of **two (2) years** thereafter, unless earlier terminated by either Party upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. The confidentiality obligations in Section 3 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of **three (3) years** from the date of disclosure of the relevant Confidential Information, except that Confidential Information constituting a trade secret shall be protected for as long as it qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.

## 7. Return or Destruction

Upon written request of the Discloser, or upon termination of this Agreement, the Recipient shall, at the Discloser's option, return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession (including all copies, extracts, and derivative materials), and certify such return or destruction in writing. The Recipient may retain one (1) archival copy solely for legal compliance purposes, which shall remain subject to this Agreement indefinitely.

## 8. No Obligation; No Partnership

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to enter into any further agreement, transaction, or business relationship. This Agreement does not create any partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between the Parties. Each Party shall bear its own costs in connection with the Purpose.

## 9. No Warranty

All Confidential Information is provided "**AS IS**", without any warranty of any kind, express or implied. Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any errors in or omissions from the Confidential Information.
